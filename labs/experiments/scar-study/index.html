Scar Study

A scar is a major drawdown from a prior all-time high, open until that high is reclaimed. Real scars and cracks in a cross-section are hairline-thin (see the radial fissures in any fire-scarred section). These are teardrops that taper to a point at both ends when recovered, open at the bark when not — at two thinnesses to calibrate.
