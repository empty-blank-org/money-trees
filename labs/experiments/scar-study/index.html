Scar Study

A scar is a major drawdown from a prior all-time high, open until that high is reclaimed. This keeps the current radial seam but reshapes its width: a recovered wound tapers to a point at both ends (a closed lens); an unrecovered one tapers only at the trough and stays open to the bark.
