Eccentric Growth

Real tree rings aren't circles — a trunk leans into wind, light, or slope, and that same direction gets favored year after year, so the eccentricity drifts slowly instead of resetting. The shipped renderer re-randomizes each ring's wobble independently, so a wild year's warp has no memory next year. This carries a persistent (direction, magnitude) forward instead: direction drifts a little each year rather than re-rolling, magnitude is a leaky integrator of that year's realized volatility — a rough year pushes the tree further off-round than it already was, calm years relax it back toward (not snap to) round. Inner rings, formed in the tree's early and more volatile years, should show more of that carried-forward irregularity; outer rings stay closer to round, same as the shipped renderer, since this is a modest, tuning pass on top of the existing per-year wobble rather than an attempt at a fully non-circular bark edge.
